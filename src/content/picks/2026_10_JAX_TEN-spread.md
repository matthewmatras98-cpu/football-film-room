Right now, the Jaguars look like the best team in football, and the numbers agree. Take offensive EPA per play and subtract what the defense allows, and Jacksonville ranks first in the league over its last four games and second for the season, just behind San Francisco. Tennessee ranks 26th on that same measure. It's hard to find a bigger gap on this week's schedule.

Trevor Lawrence and Liam Coen have the offense cooking. Lawrence is producing 0.28 EPA per dropback, second-best among starters this season, and he's stayed there over the last month. Jacksonville converts 46% of its third downs, third in the league. That's a bad matchup for a Titans defense that ranks 30th on third downs over its last four games, letting opponents convert 52% of the time. It's also allowing explosive plays on 10% of snaps. I think the Jaguars move the ball at will.

The Jags defense might be the bigger edge. It's allowed −0.157 EPA per play over the last month, third in the league, and opponents have hit explosive plays on just 5% of snaps. That's second-best. Now it gets Cam Ward, who's at −0.12 EPA per dropback this season, 25th among starters.

Give the Titans credit for the Cincinnati game. They put up 36 points in a seven-point loss, their best offensive showing of the season. But they're coming off a bye, and they traded away key defensive players, so this isn't the same team. Tennessee is also short-handed: LBs Cody Barton and Anthony Hill Jr., G Peter Skoronski, and WR Carnell Tate are all out. I think the "everybody doubts us" energy lasts about one week.

I expect Jacksonville to get out early. The Jags have led at halftime in six of eight games, including 24–0, 28–0, and 17–3. The Titans have trailed at halftime in seven of eight. Tennessee is the slowest-paced offense in the league over its last four games, and a slow offense has a hard time coming back from 14 down.

The pick: Jaguars −10.5. The better team on both sides of the ball, against a Titans team that's 1–7 and just got thinner on defense. I like Jacksonville to get ahead early and stay ahead.
