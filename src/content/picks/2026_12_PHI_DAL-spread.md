It's a division game on Thanksgiving, on the road, and 8.5 feels like a lot of points. I don't trust the Cowboys defense, and I think the Eagles defense can keep this one close.

Dallas ranks last in the league in EPA allowed this season and last on third down, where opponents convert 47.5% of the time. They're 29th in success rate allowed and 26th in explosive plays allowed, and they're still 28th in EPA allowed over the last four. This defense just gave up 46 to San Francisco and 30 to Tennessee. The Cowboys are 5–6 because their offense keeps bailing them out. It's 3rd in EPA, Dak Prescott ranks 7th in EPA per dropback, and they've scored 30, 27, 40, and 51 over their last four. That's a team built for shootouts, not weekly blowouts. They've won by 9 or more in only 3 of 11 games.

The Eagles defense isn't a shutdown unit either, and I want to be straight about that. It's 25th in EPA allowed this season, and Dallas put up 38 in Philadelphia back in week 7. But over the last two weeks this defense has kept games tight, beating the Giants 19–11 and Pittsburgh 19–16 while holding both offenses to negative EPA. If that version shows up on a short week, 8.5 is plenty of room.

The offense is the messy part. Joe Milton is starting, after Jalen Hurts started the first five games and Tanner McKee the next three. Milton ranks 30th among starters in both EPA per dropback and CPOE, and the offense is 29th in EPA and last in success rate. I'm not saying the Eagles are the better team. I just think 8.5 is a lot to lay in a division game against the league's worst defense. Both teams are on four days' rest, and Dallas is just as likely to get dragged into a shootout as to roll to a 20-point win.

The pick: Philadelphia +8.5 on the road Thursday.
