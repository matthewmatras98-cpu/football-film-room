The Jets keep losing in the margins, and Josh Allen is the wrong quarterback to give extra chances. New York is 2–7, and five of those losses were by one score. The Jets lose to good and bad teams alike, and they're especially bad at the end of halves. They've been outscored 33–6 in the final two minutes of halves this season, second-worst in the league. Right before halftime the gap is 27–3. Buffalo is +18 in that same window before the half (34–16). That's the kind of edge a quarterback like Allen turns into points.

Allen hasn't been at his best lately, but he's still producing about 0.16 EPA per dropback over his last four games and has been one of the league's most efficient passers all year. Buffalo's offense ranks third in EPA per play this season and fifth over the last month. Over the Jets' last four games, their defense has been one of the worst in football. It's allowed 0.178 EPA per play, 31st in the league, and opponents are converting 53% of third downs against it. It's hard to get off the field like that, especially against Allen.

The Bills defense is also coming into form. It's been roughly average for the season, 17th in EPA per play allowed, but over the last four games it's up to eighth at −0.097. It's allowing just 6.9% explosive plays and 36% on third downs over that stretch. Baltimore managed 10 points against it, and Minnesota managed 6.

I know laying a big number in a division game is risky. The Jets offense isn't dead: Geno Smith has been efficient over the last month, and that offense is about league average in EPA per play. But it stalls in the red zone, where New York scores touchdowns on just 29% of trips, one of the worst rates in football. Field goals won't keep pace with Buffalo.

That's why I see this game the way I do. Buffalo hasn't trailed at halftime once this season: it's led six times and been tied twice. It's outscored opponents 130–88 in first halves, while the Jets have been outscored 105–79. If the Bills get ahead early, the Jets have to play from behind and throw into a deficit. And the Jets haven't come back from those deficits this year.

**The pick:** Bills −8. Buffalo has the better quarterback, a defense that's trending up, and a big edge in the moments the Jets keep losing. I expect them to build a lead and stay ahead.
